About Marian Trolls

Marian Trolls is a social media platform dedicated to Marian College. It features humorous content, memes, and light-hearted criticism related to college life.

The page is run anonymously by a team of admins. When admins graduate, we select new students from the college to take over their roles, ensuring the legacy of the page continues.

The new admins join the existing team, and all members remain anonymous.

Our History

2016

Marian Trolls was started in 2016 by two MBA students from MIIM.

What started as a student-driven social media page has continued through different generations of Marian students.

10 Years. One Legacy.

The Admins

8

As of now, the team consists of 8 admins, including the two original founders.

The identity of every admin remains anonymous. As students graduate, new students are selected to continue the page and its legacy.
